## DETAILED DAY‑BY‑DAY PLAN  

### Day 1 – Arrival in Istanbul  
- **Morning:** Land at Istanbul Airport (IST). Transfer by private car (≈ 45 min) to Four Seasons Istanbul at the Bosphorus.  
- **Afternoon:** Light lunch at the hotel’s **Bebek** terrace with Bosphorus views. Walk to **Dolmabahçe Palace** (15 min).  
- **Evening:** Welcome dinner at **Mikla** (rooftop, modern Turkish‑Scandinavian). Early night – jet‑lag relief.

### Day 2 – Sultanahmet Classics  
- **08:00** – Turkish coffee & Turkish‑style breakfast at **Saray Muhallebicisi**.  
- **09:00** – Guided tour: **Hagia Sophia**, **Blue Mosque**, **Basilica Cistern**.  
- **12:30** – Lunch at **Karaköy Lokantası** (classic meze).  
- **14:00** – Explore **Topkapi Palace** (including Harem).  
- **17:30** – Sunset Bosphorus cruise (private yacht or public ferry).  
- **20:00** – Dinner at **Neolokal** (farm‑to‑table, UNESCO‑approved dishes).  

### Day 3 – Flight to Cappadocia & First Impressions  
- **06:30** – Transfer to Istanbul Airport, flight to **Kayseri (ASR)** (≈ 1 h 20 min).  
- **08:30** – Private shuttle to **Argos in Cappadocia** (Uçhisar, 1 h 15 min). Check‑in, freshen up.  
- **12:00** – Lunch at **Seki Restaurant** (hotel).  
- **14:00** – Walk through **Uçhisar Castle** and **Pigeon Valley**.  
- **18:00** – Sunset at **Red Valley** (short hike).  
- **20:00** – Dinner at **Ziggy’s Café** in Ürgüp (small plates, local wine).

### Day 4 – Balloon & Göreme  
- **05:00** – **Hot‑air‑balloon** flight (book with **Royal Balloon** – includes champagne toast).  
- **08:00** – Return to hotel, breakfast buffet.  
- **10:00** – Guided tour of **Göreme Open‑Air Museum** (rock‑cut churches).  
- **13:00** – Lunch in **Göreme** at **Topdeck Cave Restaurant**.  
- **15:00** – Visit **Kaymakli Underground City** (guided).  
- **18:30** – Return to hotel, relax in the terrace pool.  
- **20:00** – Dinner at **Lil’a** (Michelin‑star, fine‑dining).  

### Day 5 – Konya (Sufism)  
- **07:00** – Private transfer to **Konya** (≈ 3 h 30 min).  
- **11:00** – Check‑in at **Hagia Sophia Mansions Istanbul** (the property has a Konya‑branch; if not, stay at **Mövenpick Hotel Konya** – same level).  
- **12:30** – Lunch at **Somat** (regional Anatolian).  
- **14:00** – Visit **Mevlana Museum** (Rumi’s tomb).  
- **16:30** – Walk through **Alaeddin Hill** and **Seljuk tiles museum**.  
- **19:30** – Sema (Whirling Dervish) ceremony (book in advance).  
- **21:00** – Light dinner at hotel; early night.

### Day 6 – Pamukkale (Thermal Terraces)  
- **07:00** – Flight from Konya to **Denizli‑Çardak (DNZ)** (≈ 1 h 15 min) – or private coach (≈ 5 h).  
- **09:30** – Transfer to **Six Senses Kaplankaya** (≈ 1 h 30 min) – check‑in.  
- **12:00** – Lunch at hotel (Mediterranean).  
- **14:00** – Guided visit to **Pamukkale travertines** and **Hierapolis ancient city** (Roman theatre, Necropolis).  
- **18:00** – Optional dip in **Cleopatra’s Pool** (thermal water).  
- **20:00** – Dinner at **Mithra Restaurant** (on‑site, Turkish‑Mediterranean).  

### Day 7 – Day‑Trip to Aphrodisias & Return to Bodrum  
- **08:00** – Early breakfast, depart for **Aphrodisias** (≈ 2 h drive).  
- **10:30** – Guided tour of the **Temple of Aphrodite**, **stadium**, **museum**.  
- **13:30** – Picnic lunch (pre‑packed).  
- **15:00** – Return to hotel, stop at **Köyceğiz** for a short lakeside walk.  
- **18:00** – Back at Six Senses – enjoy spa or beach.  
- **20:00** – Dinner at **Kaplankaya’s “Mare”** (seafood).  

### Day 8 – Transfer to Ephesus (Selçuk)  
- **07:30** – Private transfer to **Selçuk** (≈ 4 h).  
- **12:00** – Check‑in at **Aqua Resort Bodrum** (or **Ephesus Palace** for a more central stay).  
- **13:00** – Lunch at **Köy Restaurant** (local dishes).  
- **15:00** – Light afternoon walk in **Selçuk town** – browse the bazaar.  
- **18:00** – Sunset at **Ayasuluk Hill** (great view over the town).  
- **20:00** – Dinner at **Mavi Balık** (seafood by the harbor).  

### Day 9 – Ancient Ephesus & Şirince  
- **08:00** – Breakfast, then **full‑day guided tour** of **Ephesus**: Library of Celsus, Great Theatre, Temple of Artemis, Terrace Houses.  
- **12:30** – Lunch in **Selçuk** at **Mehmet’s Kebab** (quick bite).  
- **14:00** – Drive to **Şirince** (30 min) – charming hillside village famous for wine and olive oil.  
- **15:30** – Wine tasting at **Şirince Vineyards** and stroll through stone‑lane boutiques.  
- **18:00** – Return to hotel, relax by the pool.  
- **20:00** – Dinner at **Bodrum’s “Köy”** (farm‑to‑table, organic).  

### Day 10 – Fly to Antalya (Turquoise Coast)  
- **07:00** – Transfer to **Bodrum Milas Airport (BJV)**, flight to **Antalya (AYT)** (≈ 1 h 15 min).  
- **09:30** – Private transfer to **Six Senses Kocatas Mansions** (luxury cliff‑side resort). Check‑in.  
- **12:00** – Lunch at the resort’s **“Kocatas”** restaurant (Mediterranean).  
- **14:00** – Guided walk through **Kaleiçi** (Old Town) – Hadrian’s Gate, **Yivli Minare**, **Hıdırlık Tower**.  
- **17:30** – Sunset at **Düden Waterfalls** (short drive).  
- **20:00** – Dinner at **7 Mehmet** (renowned Anatolian cuisine).  

### Day 11 – Beach & Leisure Day  
- **08:00** – Breakfast on the terrace.  
- **09:30** – Free time: choose between **Lara Beach**, **Konyaaltı Beach**, or a **boat trip** to **Kekova** (sunken city).  
- **13:00** – Lunch at **Rokka** (beachside café).  
- **15:00** – Spa session at Six Senses (Turkish hammam + aromatherapy).  
- **18:30** – Sunset cocktail at **Mikla Antalya** (rooftop).  
- **20:30** – Farewell dinner at **Seraser Fine Dining** (modern Turkish).  

### Day 12 – Departure  
- **Morning:** Leisurely breakfast, pack, check‑out.  
- **Transfer:** Private car to **Antalya Airport** (≈ 30 min).  
- **Flight:** Connect via Istanbul or direct to **Melbourne** (most itineraries route through Istanbul).

## 📌 Practical Tips & How to Book  

1. **Flights** – Use a flight‑search engine (Google Flights, Skyscanner) and filter for “Melbourne → Istanbul” with the airlines above. Choose a **morning arrival** on Day 1.  
2. **Domestic Connections** – Book the internal Turkish flights (Melbourne → Kayseri, Konya → Denizli, Bodrum → Antalya) **through Turkish Airlines** or its partners; they often allow a single “multi‑city” ticket, which can save ~AU$150‑200.  
3. **Transfers** –  
   * Istanbul airport → hotel: private car (≈ AU$70).  
   * Kayseri → Cappadocia, Denizli → Pamukkale, Bodrum → Antalya: most hotels arrange a **door‑to‑door shuttle** (usually included in the room rate).  
4. **Balloon Ride** – Book **early** (at least 4 weeks before) as slots fill quickly, especially in high season (May‑Oct).  
5. **Sema Ceremony in Konya** – Reserve through the **Mevlana Cultural Center**; they provide a short introductory talk and the performance (≈ AU$30).  
6. **Travel Insurance** – Recommended for medical coverage, flight cancellations, and balloon‑flight weather‑related refunds.  
7. **Visas** – Australian citizens need an **e‑Visa** for Turkey (valid for 90 days, multiple entry). Apply online (www.evisa.gov.tr) – it’s a quick 5‑minute process, cost ≈ AU$30.  
8. **Currency** – Turkish Lira (TRY). ATMs are plentiful; consider a **no‑foreign‑transaction‑fee card**.  
9. **Health & Safety** – Tap water is not generally drunk; stick to bottled water. The country is safe for solo travelers and families; standard precautions (keep passports, avoid isolated night streets) apply.
